Our Story

AbiYarn started as a passion project — a love for yarn, color, and the meditative joy of crocheting. What began as handmade gifts for family and friends quickly grew into a small business rooted in creativity and craftsmanship.

Every bouquet, flower, blanket, and custom piece is made by hand with careful attention to detail. We believe that handmade items carry a warmth and intention that mass-produced goods simply can't replicate.

Based in the Philippines, we ship our creations with love — each one wrapped and ready to bring a smile to whoever receives it.
