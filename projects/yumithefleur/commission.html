Terms of Service

Turn over

5–10 business days depending on complexity and queue.

Payment

GCash, Paypal, PayMaya, Vgen & Load (Smart) accepted. Whole Payment after the sketch is approved.

Usage Rights

Commercial use is 2× of the total price.

Revision

Free revisions are included during the work-in-progress stage. Revisions requested after the artwork is finished will require an additional fee of 10% of the total price.

Background

Basic Background are free, detailed background + 10–50% of the total price. It depends on the details.

Credits

I'll send the artwork without watermark but client should credit my page @Yumi the fleur.

Watermarks

In contradiction to not putting watermark, I'm allowed to post the artwork and use it as examples, unless you paid for commercial use.
